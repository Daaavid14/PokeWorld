Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account:

  • Email address (required for authentication)
  • Trainer username (chosen by you)
  • Google profile information (only if you use Google OAuth)

We also collect gameplay data: Pokémon owned, battle statistics, and POKÉ token balance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and secure your account
  • To display your trainer profile and stats
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset)
  • To send waitlist notifications (only if you opted in)

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (PostgreSQL). We enforce:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) — you can only access your own data
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit
  • Bcrypt-hashed passwords (handled by Supabase Auth)

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • PokéAPI — open Pokémon data (no personal data shared)
  • Google Fonts — typography (no personal data shared)
  • Vercel — web hosting

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time through the dashboard Settings page. Upon deletion, all personal data is permanently removed.

6. Cookies

We use localStorage to store your authentication session token. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@pokeworld.app.
