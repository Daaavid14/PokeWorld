How to Play

Three simple steps to start earning in the PokéWorld universe.

🎒
01

Create Your Account

Sign up in seconds. Verify your email and get your Trainer Profile instantly. New trainers receive 3 Starter Pokémon for free.

⚔️
02

Build Your Team

Collect Pokémon from the Marketplace. Build a team of 3 and develop your battle strategy. Level up your Pokémon to unlock new abilities.

💰
03

Battle & Earn

Challenge trainers worldwide. Win battles to earn POKÉ tokens. Trade your tokens or use them to acquire rare Legendary Pokémon.
